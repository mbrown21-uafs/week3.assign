Academics

River Valley College offers programs designed to prepare students with the knowleedge and skills for their futre career.

Enginerring

The Engineering program provides students with a strong foundation in mathematics, science, design, and problem-solving. Students develop communication, critical thinking, and patient-care skills while preparing for the responisbilities of a professional healtcare enviorment.

Healthcare

The healthcare program prepares students to care for others while building confidence and professional skills. Students learn about responsibility, communication, and handling the daily challenges of working in a healthcare enviorment.

Business

The Business program helps students develop leadership, finacial, communication, and decision-making skills. Students learn how businesses operate and gain knowledge that can help them build, manage, and grow a successful business.
