Admissions

River Valley College welcomes students who are ready to begin their educational journey and prepare for their future. Our admissions process is designed to help students take the first steps towards joining our supportive community and achieving their academic and career goals.

Admission Requirements

  • High school diploma or equivalent
  • Completed applicaiton
  • Official high school transcrips

How to Apply

To Apply to River Valley College, students should complete the online application and submit their official high school transcrips. Once all materials have been recieved, the admissions team will review the application and provide information about the next steps.
